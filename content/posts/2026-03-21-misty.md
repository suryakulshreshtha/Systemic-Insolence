Woke up today to the mist. It is drizzling and the air is wet and cool.

A pigeon has taken shelter on the balcony. Puffed up, eyes closed, it did not
notice me for a long while. It was not enjoying the weather the way I was, but
it did not complain either.

A very handsome rose has bloomed. Was it there yesterday?

Today's cup is warm. A good day for it.
