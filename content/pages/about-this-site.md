---
title: About this site
description: How this site is built and what it does and does not do.
---

This site is a set of static files generated from plain text. Writing is
Markdown, photographs and book covers are image files, and the pages are built
by a Ruby on Rails program and published on GitHub Pages.

## What it does not do

- It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
- It loads no scripts, fonts or styles from other websites. The only script is
  a few lines that remember whether you picked the dark or light theme.
- Photographs are published with their camera metadata (including location)
  removed.

## Feeds

New posts are available in the [feed](/feed.xml). Where a section such as the
bookshelf or the photo journal pulls from an outside service, only public
information from that service is used.
